  • 3 of 9 roles (33%) expect hands-on benchmarking/profiling tooling experience (Nsight Systems/Compute, PyTorch Profiler, custom benchmark suites).
  • 3 of 9 roles (33%) expect production reliability/observability ownership — monitoring, SLOs, tail latency (p95/p99).
  • 2 of 9 roles (22%) require GPU networking / multi-GPU communication depth — RDMA, NCCL, InfiniBand, NVLink.
  • 2 of 9 roles (22%) explicitly call out familiarity with open-source inference runtimes (vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM).
